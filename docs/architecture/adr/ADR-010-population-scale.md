# ADR-010: Population scale: one sampling fraction; 5,000 profiled people

**Status:** Proposed. **Needs Soren's approval:** it refines the agreed "about 5,000 people".

## Context

Decided: 2,000 open jobs at any point and about 5,000 people, stratified [BR decisions]. The data track says to size people "by supply, not by jobs, so that the supply and demand ratio is an output" [LD 11.4].

The arithmetic [ASM-01]:
- UK vacancy stock about 702,000 (June to August 2026) [LD 6]; the three sectors' share is not yet extracted, assumed 25% to 35%, about 210,000. So 2,000 openings are **about 1 in 105**.
- Workforce jobs M 3,504,000 + N 2,952,000 + J 1,602,000 + K 1,110,000 = **9,168,000** [LD 2]. At 1 in 105: **about 87,000 people**.
- At 1 in 105, active searchers and the laid-off (8% + 2.5% [CH 1.2]) alone are about 9,200, and the quietly looking about 14,800.

So 5,000 people against 2,000 jobs would run at a sampling fraction about 17 times smaller than the jobs. The market would be impossibly tight, every job would wait, and the supply and demand ratio, time to fill and match percentage would all be artefacts.

## Decision

- **One sampling fraction for people and jobs**, set by the population release.
- **Two depths:**
  - **Structured** (about 87,000 [ASM-01]): identity, truth, structured CV, signal observations; generated by code at negligible cost.
  - **Profiled** (5,000, the agreed number): CV prose, evidence a CV never shows, onboarding findings; eligible for archetype and live tiers; stratified to oversample in-market states and rare cells, with design weights [ASM-20].
- Rule-tier runs use everyone; LLM tiers draw from the profiled 5,000.

## Alternatives considered

- **5,000 people only, plus an aggregate background pool** (cell-level counts competing for jobs through a matching function). Keeps 5,000 records, fixes tightness, but loses individual fit, tenure and shoehorning for most hires. The fallback if Soren prefers it.
- **Scale jobs down to match 5,000 people** (about 115 jobs). Breaks the agreed 2,000 and the per-cell floors. Rejected.
- **Different fractions with weights only.** Weights fix reported shares, not the dynamics of who competes for which job. Rejected.

## Consequences

- Tightness, time to fill and the supply and demand ratio are real outputs.
- Compute grows (pair scoring is role-gated; a rule-tier run is still minutes [ASM-25]); LLM cost does not grow, because authoring and live agents use the profiled 5,000.
- The vacancy share needs VACS02 by section to replace the assumption.
