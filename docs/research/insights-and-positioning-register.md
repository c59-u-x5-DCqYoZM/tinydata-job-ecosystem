# Insights and positioning register

**This is a running register, kept current.** Soren asked for "a complete running register of what are the um insights and compelling positioning pieces that we're going to go test on each side of this of this proposition" (5 October 2026; full words in `product/job-ecosystem/2026-10-05-synthetic-job-ecosystem-brief.md`).

**How it works:**

- **One row per insight or positioning piece**, for the individual side (I), the producer side (P) or both (B).
- **Every row has:**
  - a source
  - an evidence status: *Claim* (stated, untested), *Evidence* (external data supports it), *Modelled* (the job ecosystem shows it), *Tested* (a consumer or producer test confirmed it), or *Killed*
  - the test that would confirm or kill it
- **Nothing here is ratified** until Soren marks it so. Soren's own words are quoted, never paraphrased.
- **Who updates it:** the morning project-manager update reports changes; the job ecosystem and the consumer tests move rows along.
